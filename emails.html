Day 3. One complete, do-it-today tactic given away free, with a per-department checklist attached. The pitch is four words in the P.S.

Yesterday, I showed you what you need to graduate from Stage 4.

(If you haven't opened your Stage 4 PDF yet, open it by clicking here.)

The main problem at Stage 4 is that you've been saying “yes” to any customer who would pay you.

Now that's causing problems because different customers want different things.

You need to figure out who your best customers are and focus on them.

The Solution: The 80/20 Customer Audit

In most businesses, 80% of customers take up the most headaches and resources. While also giving the lowest return.

The top 20% are the ones to replicate.

They pay more. They complain less. They take less effort to fulfill. They get better results. They refer more often.

When you isolate your best customers, you'll see a pattern.

They tend to share the same type of business and they need the same type of delivery.

Those top 20% customers are what you're going to build the business around.

Do this:

Open a sheet and list every customer from the last 12 months.

Give each customer a ranking of 1-10 on each of the following:

- Total revenue paid - Ease of fulfillment - Satisfaction with your service

Sort by revenue and write down what the top customers have in common (industry, size, urgency, use case, outcome).

Pay close attention to the ones with high revenue, low ease of fulfilment, and high satisfaction with your service.

Those are the customers you need to clone.

Your job is to get more customers exactly like them and optimize your product around that type of customer.

That is your focus this week…

Bonus: Stage 4 Wins By Department

Product: Specialize product and price to serve your niched down avatar. Marketing: Make better free stuff and add more creatives to boost volume. Sales: Track basic KPIs manually on a spreadsheet. Get a project management tool and a work chat tool. Recruiting: Learn how to screen and conduct a 2-step interview process. HR: Create an employee handbook and policies. Finance: Get profit and loss statements set up. Get basic insurance.
