Three things the pages give away

1. The price is on the page, and so is the floor

“Tickets are $5k per seat.” Then, immediately: “If you are under 250k/yr this is not a fit (yet.)” and “If you haven’t made at least $5k from the free content, don’t do it.”

That last line is doing three jobs at once. It disqualifies, so the setter never argues about budget. It flexes — the free stuff is worth $5k on its own. And it reframes the price as a proven multiple rather than a cost. VERIFIED

2. They justify the price with their staff’s salaries

“you work with our directors that are paid $350k, $600k, $900k+ per year.” Not “world-class experts”. Numbers. It is an unfakeable, checkable, specific claim, and it makes $5,000 for two days of their time read as cheap. VERIFIED

3. There is no guarantee anywhere

No refund policy, no money-back, no risk reversal of any kind on either sales page. The entire risk reversal is social: the room, the salaries, the sold-out proof, and the industry list. VERIFIED — searched both captures.
