They sell a $5,000 seat by making you fail an application for something else

Alex and Leila Hormozi run no webinar, no VSL funnel and almost no paid acquisition for this. The machine is four complete free courses and a book that has sold 1.4 million copies, feeding a HubSpot list that gets mailed every two days forever — on an evergreen loop that repeats on an exact 56-day clock.

The close is the part worth stealing. Their application is not an application for the workshop. It is an application to become a portfolio company — for investment. You answer revenue and profit three separate ways, and when your numbers come up short the form tells you so and offers the paid workshop as the way to fix it. The $5,000 seat is the consolation prize for failing at something more prestigious.

Will is inside this funnel personally. He bought $100M Money Models in August 2025 and has booked their call twice — Braeden Bowman in May 2026 and Coleman Burgess in July 2026. The whole sequence, both setter threads and 77 broadcast emails, is sitting in his Gmail.
